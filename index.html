OUR STORY

A legacy of trust since 1993

Nataraj Oil Mills Private Limited (NOMPL), established in 1993, is the visionary brainchild of our founder, Mr. KSR Natarajan, in Madurai. Our family's rich heritage in sesame oil production spans generations, combining traditional expertise with modern innovation.

With the strategic insights and contemporary ideas of his sons, Mr. Natarajan transformed this legacy into a thriving company that has since become a household name in essential food products.

In 2025, Anjali Retail Private Limited was launched as a subsidiary to expand our footprint in the retail sector through franchising and Anjali Retail Store in Rajakilpakkam brings that manufacturing heritage directly to your neighbourhood.
