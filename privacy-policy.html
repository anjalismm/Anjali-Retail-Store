Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Anjali Retail Store, a retail venture of Nataraj Oil Mills Private Limited, located in Rajakilpakkam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Information we collect

This website does not have accounts, logins or a checkout, and we do not ask you to submit personal details through a form on this site.

When you contact us through WhatsApp or by telephone, you share the information you choose to send us — typically your name, phone number, delivery address and shopping list. We use that information only to prepare, confirm and deliver your order.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and reliability purposes.

Cookies and measurement

We use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel to understand how visitors find and use this website, and to measure the results of our advertising. These services set cookies and may collect information such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, approximate location, device and browser.

We record when visitors click Visit Store, Get Directions, WhatsApp, Call and offer links. Some of this measurement is sent from your browser and some from our own server, which also passes on your IP address, browser type and the advertising identifiers described above. We do not send your name, address, telephone number or order contents to these services, and this website does not collect them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. The website works normally either way.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers who help us run this website and our advertising — currently our hosting provider, Google and Meta — and where we are required to do so by law.

Third-party services

This site embeds a Google Maps view of our store location and links out to WhatsApp, Google, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you interact with them.

Keeping information

Order-related messages are retained only as long as we need them to serve you and to keep our own business records. Measurement data is retained according to the standard retention settings of Google Analytics and Meta.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
